The Final Goodbye

If you are reading this, then it means I am gone. But I still wanted to share my story, maybe finally I will be heard, even if it falls on deaf ears.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

I rarely had friends growing up. In fact, I could easily remember times where I never had any at all. I was bullied no matter what for how differently I functioned from all the other kids, all through out elementary and middle school with the same bullies transitioning from middle into most of high school. My parents did intervene once in elementary, but that did nothing as I would only be bullied again and again in both middle and high school. I would often take my anger out on everyone else instead, since nothing could be done. It made me realize I cannot fit in this society. I had only ever known sadness, hatred, and despair. I am a ghost, a lesser human. So I locked myself away, and threw away the key to who I really am. The only true escape I had were video games. My family always thought it was a phase, it wasn’t.

I was always alone. No one to help me at all, no one to care for what I was going through. That it is all in my head. That it is all of my own doing. I felt alone in this world for the vast majority of my life. This hole in my heart had been tearing me apart for years, ripping away what was left. By Fall of 2025, it only worsened. I tried cutting myself several times during the entire semester. I would constantly fall into distress as I held the knife up to my neck, but I was too sensitive to the pain and eventually stopped. The amount of times I just wanted to end my life that semester, the looks were in my eyes too and no one noticed. Why would they? No one would or could care less, there wasn't anyone I could reliably go to. I rarely ask for help because it is never reciprocated, such was the case in December, 2025. I was let down like I always am, told to fend for myself. And, this was for a competition required me to answer a simple question that a failure like me could never do right. I could barely socialize and function like normal human being as I often had to put up an act to fit in, yet there were high expectations placed on me. Everyone had someone they can go to, I have no one. Or had no one in this case. It was something that could've changed my life for the better, but I was let down while anguish and doubt took hold. I needed help and it wasn't just for the competition, with no one I can go to at all, not a single soul.

It was only when the new year of 2026 begin that I would inevitably decide to let go. Let go of everything, as I was left alone and will always be alone, accepting that fact. Eating less and less as soon as January started. I began losing sleep since the year began, frantic panic attacks and anxious breakdowns constant. Through out the spring semester I just wanted it to be over, but I kept delaying it out of fear when I should've just committed already. I eventually became weak, and often couldn't stand for long. I didn't know how I should I do it. I thought about drowning in Lake Claire, but that would take a lot of effort and caused too much pain. Maybe suicide by cop, grabbing their gun to blow my own head off or somehow get into a fake standoff. No one could tell what I was going through, even with my hands trembling the entire Spring semester. As if anyone would care if they did. This continued until I tried VSED between March and April. I rarely ate or drank, up until I could feel my kidneys causing me pain along with a tear forming on my heart. Even my lungs gave out a few times, while the nights grew colder. But once the VSED neared fatal, I tried to hang myself late that night, April 19th, with whatever I could get my hands on.

However, I couldn't just couldn't go through with it no matter how badly I wanted to. Thus, I waited until April 21st with what little strength I had left to see someone I thought really cared for me. This was as the Spring semester was nearing the end and I might not survive. That was a big mistake. I couldn't sit still and my hands were shaking, with the trembles it was impossible for my index finger to remain straight. And, the person I needed the most in my life at that time was not interested which is why I never said a word prior as I held it all in, they had other priorities and did not care for what I really had to say. Kind of ironic, isn't it? Going to ask for help finally, after years of silence, only to be let down and told classes will begin soon? Although, I did choose to cheer her up instead and should've been more open about the depression, the isolation, social anxiety, and all of the demons I've been harboring. So the fault lies with me as one whole, not to the other. She meant the world to me, a safe harbor from this sickening planet. The warmth I lacked in this cold universe. It was at that moment, I realized I am too far gone.

Fast forward, over the Summer semester, everything just kept eating me alive along with my weakened body. Every night has always been a curse to me, memories all flooding in and the only way I ever slept is if I cried or pray that my body would produce enough melatonin to force a shutdown. No matter what, my sanity slowly disappeared. The hypothermia only made it harder to move and think for the whole summer. By the beginning of August, I was ready to just give up as the one person I depended on to pull me out of the storm I'm in, departed before I could say a word in person. Even when I needed them the most, again. I just needed a guide, a light to help me see in the pitch black prison that is my mind.

Sometimes I wondered... "Why me? Why always me?" Maybe it was always meant to end this way. I saw the future once, and I was never in it. I was different, and looked down upon for it; I did the next best thing and became the joke. I chose to make people laugh because everyone would laugh at me anyways if I showed my true self. I just wanted to feel alive and happy. Not broken. Not alone. Like I'm not different and actually belonged. I hated myself, yet if I did admit it all, I'd have been told to go to therapy, to find help, or be locked away for my life to erode in chaos and misery. Even by the person I needed the most at that time. I just needed someone, anyone, to push and pull me out. To change my mind and help me embrace this world. Help me feel something, even if it's just for a split second. Save me from myself, save me from doing something stupid.

I couldn't concentrate and it always bothered me how just being in environments with any sort of crowd gave me headaches. I always had to mimic people to fit in, sometimes I was considered an idiot for not understanding social cues. Like I am not normal at all and nothing more than a freak. I thought about doing it often, plotting for years, dreaming about killing myself. By Fall 2026, I had no one I really trusted with all of this. So what is the point of going on? Too much lost opportunity has happened, too much regret. Maybe I was always a burden to everyone, and that is why I never got the help, both needed and wanted. Maybe that is why I was turned away. It is the only thing that made sense.

I refuse to stay in a place I don't belong, with no one there when I need them. I know my parents and my sister tried their best, but I cannot stay here any longer. Even if I were the one to reach out instead, I would be the one burned in the end. I can't do this anymore. No one cares about you or what you were going through unless you're gone, and that is the truth. And only then, those who finally care, do so out of regret. The signs were there, they always were.

Is it too much to ask for help? I needed help far more than others, they're still here and I won't be. That is the difference.

Thanh K. Singh
